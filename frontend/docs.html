SkyFlow Architecture & Specifications

SkyFlow is an enterprise-grade, transactional Flight Management & Autonomous Booking System designed to prevent overselling, handle multi-currency live pricing, manage schedule disruptions, and automate customer-facing workflows.

2. Flight Booking Lifecycle

The booking flow guarantees atomicity and zero overselling through the following sequential pipeline:

  1. Search & Multi-Currency: Users query available flights with live class availability and choose their preferred currency (PKR, USD, EUR, AED, SAR, etc.).
  2. 15-Minute Price Hold: Users can freeze a fare quote for 15 minutes to protect against dynamic price spikes.
  3. 10-Minute Seat Hold: Selecting a seat in the interactive cabin map temporarily decrements availability with an active countdown timer.
  4. Atomic Checkout: Submits payment with transparent Idempotency-Key deduplication.
  5. E-Ticket & PNR Issuance: Generates confirmed booking reference and sends instant HTML E-Ticket via Gmail SMTP.

3. Atomic Seat Holds & Row-Level Locking

To prevent race conditions when multiple passengers attempt to book the last remaining seat simultaneously, SkyFlow implements PostgreSQL row-level locking:

SELECT * FROM flights WHERE id = :flight_id FOR UPDATE;

This blocks concurrent transactions until the active booking completes or rolls back, strictly enforcing the HARD_LIMIT overbooking policy.

4. Cancellations & Travel Credit Expiration

Passengers can perform Full Cancellation or Partial Passenger Cancellation (for multi-passenger group bookings):

  • Proportional Refund Calculation: Automatically computes refund portions while preserving remaining passengers.
  • 1-Year Travel Credit: Travel credits are issued with an explicit expires_at = NOW() + 1 Year timestamp and validated upon checkout.

5. Airline Disruption Management

When an administrator adjusts a flight departure or arrival time:

  • Minor Schedule Shifts (≤ 2 Hours): Triggers immediate cascading email alerts to all confirmed booking holders.
  • Major Disruptions (> 2 Hours): System automatically scans for alternative flights on the same route and rebooks passengers, emailing updated E-Tickets.

6. Dual-Writer Architecture (FastAPI + n8n)

FastAPI and n8n share the same PostgreSQL database as the single source of truth:

  • FastAPI: Owns all live booking and admin modification writes.
  • n8n Cloud: Executes 4 scheduled workflows:
    • 1. Check-in reminders (24h & 2h before departure).
    • 2. Waitlist auto-promotion polling (every 15 minutes).
    • 3. Stuck refund escalation alert to Super Admin (>3 days pending).
    • 4. Daily operations revenue & load factor summary report.

7. Autonomy & Human Approval Boundaries

Sensitive operations are flagged with requires_approval = true and placed into the PENDING_APPROVAL audit queue. Super Administrators can review, approve, or reject these actions with 1 click from the Operations Portal.

8. AI Policy Assistant (RAG Engine)

The floating AI Airline Assistant is powered by ChromaDB vector store and Groq LLM. It performs semantic retrieval over official airline fare rules, baggage policies, and refund guidelines to provide accurate, grounded answers to passenger queries.
